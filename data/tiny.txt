the small model learns one byte at a time.
the model reads a context and predicts the next byte.
attention lets each position look at earlier positions.
the causal mask prevents a position from seeing the future.
an embedding turns a byte into a vector of numbers.
a position embedding tells the model where the byte appears.
rms normalization keeps vector scales under control.
the feed forward network transforms every position independently.
residual connections carry old information around each transformation.
softmax turns logits into a probability distribution.
cross entropy measures how surprised the model was by the correct byte.
backpropagation sends the error through every operation in reverse order.
adam uses moving averages to update every learned parameter.
training repeats prediction, loss, backward, and update.
inference repeats prediction and sampling to create new text.
